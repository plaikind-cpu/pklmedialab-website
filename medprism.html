What MedPrism Checks
A structured multi-pass analysis covering the integrity signals that matter most.
1

Statistical Methodology Claude AI

Multiple comparisons without correction, post-hoc analyses presented as pre-specified, borderline p-values, effect size plausibility, and unusual statistical patterns that may indicate data issues.

2

Reporting Integrity Claude AI

Selective outcome reporting, outcome switching between registration and publication, inconsistencies between methods and results sections, and overclaiming relative to study design.

3

Publication Integrity Brave Search

Retraction history, journal credibility screening, predatory publisher detection, DOI verification, and cross-referencing against external integrity databases.

4

Transparency Disclosures Claude AI

Conflict of interest statements, data availability, funding disclosure, and trial or study pre-registration — assessed as informational disclosures, not as red flags.

Flagged Items
Each integrity concern is categorized by severity with a plain-language explanation and supporting excerpt from the paper.
HIGH Serious concerns that materially affect confidence in the paper — undisclosed conflicts of interest in industry-sponsored trials, evidence of data manipulation, or publication in a known predatory journal.
MEDIUM Notable concerns warranting attention — multiple comparisons without correction, post-hoc analyses, selective outcome reporting, or borderline statistical significance.
LOW Minor issues or areas of uncertainty — limited sample size relative to claims, missing standard reporting elements, or self-reported exposure data without validation.

🧩 Chrome Extension
Analyze papers directly from PubMed or any journal page — without leaving your browser.

Available on the Chrome Web Store

Click the MedPrism toolbar button on any research article page. The side panel opens alongside the paper — MedPrism detects the article automatically and retrieves the full text where available. Works on PubMed, Cell Press, PLOS, bioRxiv, medRxiv, BMJ, Nature, and most journal sites.

💡 For PubMed pages, MedPrism automatically retrieves full text from PubMed Central where available. For paywalled content, ensure you are logged in to the journal site first.

The extension requires the same access code as the web app. Request a code by email — access is free of charge.
