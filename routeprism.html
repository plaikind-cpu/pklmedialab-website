How RoutePrism Works
A multi-pass AI pipeline that turns your origin, destination, and preferences into a structured day-by-day itinerary.
1

Set Your Route & Profile You

Enter your origin, destination, and travel dates. Choose or customize a travel profile — van camping, car and hotel, or fully custom. Set daily drive limits, lodging preferences, and any must-hit waypoints.

2

AI Plans Your Waypoints Claude AI

Claude AI researches your corridor and builds a logical sequence of overnight stops and day visits — spacing drive times to match your pace, flagging National Parks, scenic routes, and points of interest along the way.

3

Each Stop is Researched Brave Search

Every waypoint is researched in real time — campground names and amenities, hotel options, must-see attractions, dining, and local tips. The result is a stop-by-stop briefing, not just a list of city names.

4

Refine & Save Interactive Map

Review your itinerary on an interactive Google Map. Add, remove, or reorder stops. Use Suggest Stops to fill gaps, or Ask AI for midpoint suggestions. Save multiple routes and load them across devices.

What You Get
A full trip-planning workspace — not just a list of stops, but a structured itinerary you can actually drive.
🗺️

Interactive Route Map

Google Maps integration with lettered markers for every stop, live driving directions, and a right-side sidebar with per-stop details and drive times.

📋

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Each day shows overnight stop, day visits, drive time from the prior stop, and AI-researched briefing notes on what to do and where to stay.

💡

Suggest Stops Panel

Ask RoutePrism to suggest additional stops along your corridor — filtered by type (parks, cities, attractions). Save multiple suggestion lists and swap them in.

📏

Distance Calculator

Instantly sum the driving miles along your route between any two stops — skipping day visits to give you actual road distance, not straight-line estimates.

🤖

Ask AI Anywhere

Ask open-ended questions mid-trip: "What's a good midpoint between here and Denver?" or "Recommend a scenic detour through Colorado." AI responds in context.

💾

Save & Sync Routes

Routes are saved server-side — load them on any device, pick up where you left off, and keep multiple trip variations in parallel.
